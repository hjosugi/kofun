Historical prototypes do not count as active after their source is removed.

The Claim column is the stable identity of each row in release/claims.json, which binds it to a positive gate, a boundary that fails outside it, and a reproduction command. make release-claims fails if a row here gains, loses, or reworders a capability without the manifest following, so this table and the evidence cannot drift apart. Status text lives here and is mirrored into the manifest; do not restate it in the manifest by hand.
